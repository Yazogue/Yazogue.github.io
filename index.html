MonPipelineTikTok

MonPipelineTikTok is a personal publishing tool used by a single creator to post Euro NCAP crash-test videos to their own TikTok account. Videos are cut to a vertical format, titled with the car and its safety rating, and credited "Source: Euro NCAP". The creator has written permission from Euro NCAP to republish this footage.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorises the tool to post on their account.
  2. The tool prepares one video per day on the creator's own computer.
  3. Using the Content Posting API, the video is sent to the creator's TikTok account with its caption. The creator chooses the privacy level and the interaction settings, as required by TikTok.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's data.
